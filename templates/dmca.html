How to Submit a Takedown Claim

If you are an author, university representative, or official copyright owner and believe a file hosted on our system infringes your copyrights, please submit a deletion query to our desk containing:

  1. The exact filename as displayed in our Available Materials list.
  2. Clear proof of your ownership or legal right to represent the material owner.
  3. Direct contact coordinates.

Send your request directly to: koechc318@gmail.com

Validated requests will be permanently deleted from our storage architecture within 24 to 48 hours.
